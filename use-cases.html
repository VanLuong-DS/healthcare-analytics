Healthcare Analytics Use Cases

These small projects show how I would turn a question into a dataset, a calculation and a useful takeaway.

1. Where are medical visits concentrated?

Question: Which diagnoses and provider specialties account for the most visits? Data: visits + providers. Approach: join on provider_id, group by diagnosis and specialty, count visits, then compare paid vs denied claims.

Try the SQL exercise

2. How do claim amounts differ?

Question: What is the average allowed amount by payer plan? Data: visits + payers. Approach: compare claim counts, mean and median amounts; review outliers before explaining differences.

Explore the statistics practice

3. Patient visit patterns

Question: Which patients have multiple visits in the sample period? Data: patients + visits. Approach: count distinct visits per patient and inspect timing. Avoid treating utilization alone as a measure of need.

Try the SQL exercise

4. Diagnosis to prescription fill

Question: How much time passes between a recorded visit and a linked prescription fill? Data: visits + treatments. Approach: link on visit_id and compare dates. Caution: the synthetic data is not designed to establish clinical appropriateness or treatment gaps.

Try the SQL exercise

5. Build a one-page summary

Question: What should a nontechnical audience know about utilization in this sample? Approach: lead with one headline, two charts, definitions and limitations. Show counts alongside percentages.
