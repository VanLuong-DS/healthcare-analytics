Data dictionary

providers: provider_id, specialty, region

payers: plan_id, plan_type, plan_name

visits: visit_id, patient_id, provider_id, plan_id, visit_date, diagnosis_code, place_of_service, allowed_amount, claim_status

treatments: rx_id, patient_id, visit_id, plan_id, fill_date, drug_name, days_supply, allowed_amount

Some generated relationships and dates are intentionally simplified and may not follow realistic clinical sequences.
