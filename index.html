Build your skills, one step at a time.

Start with the basics, develop practical tools, learn the healthcare context, and then put everything together through applied use cases. New lessons and examples can be added as the library grows.

01

Math & Statistics Basics

Learn how to summarize data, understand variation, and interpret results without getting lost in formulas.

  • Population, sample, observations, and denominators
  • Counts, percentages, proportions, and rates
  • Mean, median, quartiles, and percentiles
  • Distributions, histograms, and outliers
  • Variability, trends, correlation, and basic inference
02

Data Analytics Fundamentals

Develop practical skills for getting data, preparing it, analyzing it, and communicating what it means.

ExcelSQLPython LLMs & AIVisualization & Storytelling
  • Excel formulas, pivot tables, lookups, and data cleaning
  • SQL queries, aggregations, and joins
  • Python and pandas for reading and analyzing files
  • Using LLMs to explore data and validate results
  • Charts, dashboards, and stakeholder communication
03

Healthcare Domain Knowledge

Understand the U.S. healthcare ecosystem and the data it produces.

  • Manufacturers, distributors, pharmacies, providers, payers, and patients
  • Insurers, Medicare, Medicaid, and other key stakeholders
  • Medical claims, pharmacy claims, EHR, and public health data
  • ICD-10-CM, HCPCS, NDC, and NPI basics
  • Public datasets, data quality, and healthcare metrics
04

Use Case Inventory

Follow realistic business questions from problem definition through analysis and recommendations.

  • Prescription volume and pharmaceutical distribution
  • Healthcare utilization and claims cost analysis
  • Patient populations and service patterns
  • AI-assisted exploratory data analysis
  • End-to-end projects with charts and presentations

About me

Connecting data, analytics, AI, and healthcare.

I'm a data professional and AI enthusiast who uses AI regularly in my analytics work. I've spent close to a decade working in healthcare and the pharmaceutical industry, and I enjoy finding practical ways to use data and technology to solve problems.

What motivates me is the opportunity to contribute to better healthcare outcomes, more efficient operations, and ultimately better patient care. I started this website to share what I've learned, exchange ideas with people who have similar interests, and offer a starting point for anyone curious about healthcare analytics.

This is a place for practical knowledge, new ideas, and examples that connect analytics skills with real healthcare questions.
